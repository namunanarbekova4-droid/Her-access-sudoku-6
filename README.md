Her Access Sudoku 🧠♟️
“Access to education. Access to opportunity. Access to a great game.”
What is this?
Her Access Sudoku is not just another Sudoku app. It’s a brain-training platform built with purpose inspired by Her Access, a project dedicated to supporting girls’ education in regions where access is restricted. The idea is simple: if we fight for access to education, we should also build tools that are worth accessing. Smart, beautiful, and meaningful.
What I built
	•	🤖 AI Coach: analyzes your actual board in real time, explains why a number fits or doesn’t, and teaches real Sudoku strategies
	•	🏆 Global Leaderboard : compete with players worldwide on daily challenges
	•	📅 Daily Challenge : a new puzzle every day, the same for all users
	•	📈 Progress Tracking :tracks your time, mistakes, win rate, and best results
	•	✏️ Pencil Notes : mark candidates and plan your moves strategically
	•	🌙 Dark / Light Theme : designed to be comfortable at any time of day
Who it’s for
Anyone who wants to train their mind daily and use a product that was built with care — not just copied from a template. Behind every design decision is a belief that good tools should be accessible to everyone, everywhere.
Why it’s different
Most Sudoku apps exist. This one means something. It combines competitive features, an AI tutor, and a design that feels premium - built as a real product, not a homework assignment.

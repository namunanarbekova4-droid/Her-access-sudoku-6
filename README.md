Her Access Sudoku 🧠

“Access to education. Access to opportunity. Access to a great game.”

What is this? Her Access Sudoku is not just another Sudoku app. It’s a brain-training platform built with purpose inspired by Her Access, a project dedicated to supporting girls’ education in regions where access is restricted. The idea is simple: if we fight for access to education, we should also build tools that are worth accessing. Smart, beautiful, and What I built • 🤖 AI Coach explains why a number works or doesn’t, teaches real Sudoku strategies • 🏆 Global Leaderboard : compete with players worldwide • 📅 Daily Challenge : a new puzzle every day, same for all users • 🎨 Unique Design: clean, modern, and intentional

Who it’s for Anyone who wants to train their mind daily and use a product that was built with care —not just copied from a template.

Why it’s different Most Sudoku apps exist. This one means something. Behind every design decision is a belief that good tools should be accessible to everyone
